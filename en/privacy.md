Auroravoice is a local desktop app for macOS built on one core principle: **your text and your voice stay yours**.

## What we collect

- No account required; we collect no personal information
- The app contains no telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting
- This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard access data (e.g., IP address, timestamps) under its own policy — this is unrelated to the app

## Text & audio data

- **Local engine (MLX, recommended)**: text chunking and speech synthesis run entirely on your Mac — no text or audio ever leaves your device
- **Voice cloning**: samples you record or import are stored locally only, used solely to build your personal voice
- **Reading progress & settings**: saved locally; removed when you uninstall the app

## Optional third-party engines

The following engines are optional and disabled by default:

- **Remote API**: if you configure your own API provider, text is sent to that provider for synthesis and handled under its privacy policy
- **Edge TTS**: text is sent to Microsoft servers for synthesis; see Microsoft's privacy statement for details

The app clearly warns you before any cloud engine is used.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Significant changes will be announced in-app or on the homepage.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository.
